The field guide

Support.

For Rampart Muster on iPhone. The game is preparing for its first App Store release.

Your first defense

  1. Read the briefing and choose Fortify.
  2. Place walls, towers or gates inside the gold outline. Reserve silver for troops.
  3. Choose Garrison, select Inf or Arc, set the squad size and tap a legal position.
  4. Tap a squad to edit it. Apply confirms changes; Move repositions it; Dismiss refunds its cost.
  5. Choose Begin when your setup is valid.

Move around the battlefield

Pan mode moves the map without placing anything. Use the zoom controls to inspect details and Overview to return to a broad view. The question-mark button shows or hides first-siege hints.

Why can I not begin?

Recruit at least one valid squad and resolve any setup problem shown by the Begin button. Keep enough silver for the recruitment cost. A tap outside a legal deployment position will not create a squad.

Report a problem

Contact Anton Zhdankin: antonzhdankin@gmail.com.

Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, map, what happened and the steps that reproduce it. A screenshot can help. Remove private notifications or other personal information before sending it. Never send passwords, payment-card information or Apple account credentials.

Do not delete the app as a first troubleshooting step: locally stored progress may be lost. Contact support before reinstalling. Response times and recovery of deleted progress cannot be guaranteed.
