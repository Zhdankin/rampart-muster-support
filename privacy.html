Your information

Privacy.

Effective date: October 3, 2026. This policy covers Rampart Muster version 1.0, voluntary support correspondence and this website. The game is preparing for its first App Store release.

Who is responsible

Rampart Muster is provided by Anton Zhdankin. For support or privacy questions, email antonzhdankin@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

The app stores game progress and the first-siege hints preference locally on your device. Gameplay data is not sent to the developer. The app has no in-app account, advertising, analytics SDK or third-party tracking.

Removing the app may remove its locally stored data. Device backups are controlled by your iPhone and Apple account settings; the app does not operate a separate cloud service.

When you contact support

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your message, reply address and any attachments. This information is used to answer your request, investigate the reported issue and maintain necessary support records. Sending an email is optional and is separate from playing the game. Do not include passwords, payment-card details or information unrelated to your request.

Support email is delivered through Gmail, operated by Google. Google handles email and related service information under its privacy policy. Your email provider may also process the correspondence.

Support information is retained only as needed to handle the request, related follow-up, applicable legal obligations or disputes. You may request deletion using the contact address above. Service-provider retention and backups are governed by the provider's policies; an exact deletion time for those systems cannot be guaranteed.

This website

These static pages do not add analytics scripts, advertising, third-party embedded media, tracking cookies or contact forms. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and handles hosting information under its privacy statement. GitHub controls its own log retention. The developer does not operate a separate visitor-tracking system.

Google and GitHub may process service information in other countries as described in their policies.

Apple services

App Store purchases, distribution, system backups and optional diagnostic sharing are managed by Apple under its settings and privacy information. The developer does not receive your payment-card details. If Apple makes crash or diagnostic reports available through its developer services, those reports are used to investigate reliability issues; the app does not include its own diagnostic-upload service.

Your requests

Email the contact address above to ask about, correct or request deletion of personal information held in support correspondence. Where applicable, you may also request access, restriction, objection or portability, or contact your data-protection authority. Requests are handled subject to applicable law; information needed for legal obligations or disputes may need to be retained. Requests concerning Google or GitHub's own records should also be directed to the relevant provider.

To remove game data stored only on your iPhone, use your device's app-deletion and backup controls. The developer cannot retrieve that local progress remotely. Do not delete the game merely to troubleshoot a problem.

Changes

This page and its effective date will be updated if the app, support or website data practices change. The App Store privacy answers must also be updated when applicable.
